    By aircraft type

    A row per aircraft type, with a lane per source: a dot for each flight, coloured by its source, and a tick at the lane's median. A hollow dot is at an edge of its source's data, so it may be wrong, and is left out of the median. Path angle is fitted 3 km either side of the closest approach, as adsb analyse does, and is negative for a descent. LAmax is the loudest event given to the pass, one dot each. It follows the filters and the time window above. Each lane holds whatever its source saw, and the sources cover different hours, so to compare sources, tick In several sources only: every lane then holds the same passes. Click a dot to select its pass.

    Passes

    Each pass's closest approach to the receptor in each source. Grey: the closest point is at the end of that source's track, so the aircraft may have come closer outside its data. Δ height: the first source's height minus the second's. Drag the table, or Shift-scroll, to see columns off to the right. Where a source has no flight, it says why: no data then, outside its area or above its ceiling mean it couldn't have seen the pass; missed means it should have.
